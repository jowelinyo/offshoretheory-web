  • UK and Channel Islands tide gauges: Generated using E.U. Copernicus Marine Service Information; Global Ocean - Delayed Mode Sea level product (INSITU_GLO_PHY_SSH_DISCRETE_MY_013_053), https://doi.org/10.17882/93670. Harmonic constants analysed by the course.
  • French tide gauges: sea-level observations from REFMAR, Shom (Réseaux de référence des observations marégraphiques), downloaded September 2026, Licence Ouverte 2.0 (Etalab). Harmonic constants analysed by the course; this reuse is not endorsed by the Shom.
  • French secondary ports: Shom, Références Altimétriques Maritimes (RAM), Licence Ouverte 2.0 (Etalab).
  • Secondary ports: "The FES2022 Tide product was funded by CNES, produced by LEGOS, NOVELTIS and CLS and made freely available by AVISO". CNES, 2024. FES2022 (Finite Element Solution) Tidal model (Version 2024) [Data set]. Modified from the original distribution: harmonic constants extracted at port positions and referred to chart datum.

  • Tidal streams where the Shom grid does not reach: Generated using E.U. Copernicus Marine Service Information; https://doi.org/10.48670/moi-00054
  • Coastline: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL (www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

  • Wrecks and obstructions: Contains public sector information, licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0, from the UK Hydrographic Office.
  • Lights and buoys: Shom, 2026. https://dx.doi.org/10.17183/DOI_BALISAGE_MARITIME
  • Lights: List of Lights, Radio Aids and Fog Signals, Pub. 114 — National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA).
  • Nature of the seabed: Seabed substrates, EMODnet Geology, CC-BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  • Place names: GeoNames, CC-BY 4.0, https://www.geonames.org
