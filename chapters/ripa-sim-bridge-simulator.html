The bridge simulator

You are on watch, in clear visibility, and another vessel is coming up over the horizon — sometimes two or three. Work out what she is, whether there is a risk of collision and who keeps out of the way, then handle your ship. When the encounter is over, your manoeuvre is marked against the Rules.

  • Play runs the clock, thirty times faster than real time. Pause stops it, and you can answer at any moment.
  • Take a bearing writes down her compass bearing and the time. If it hardly changes while she comes closer, there is a risk of collision.
  • Binoculars bring her closer, by night and by day. With more than one vessel in sight, choose which one with A, B or C.
  • The helm turns you 10° or 30° to port or to starboard. The engine goes to half speed, back to full, stops, or goes astern. She does not stop dead: in this exercise she gains or loses knot a minute, a minute going astern, until she makes knots astern. An order given while paused waits until you press Play.

The marking quotes the Rules, and next to them a few figures: a safe passing distance, how many minutes before the closest point of approach, how large a turn. Those figures are the criterion of this exercise, not the Rules — the Rules give none.
